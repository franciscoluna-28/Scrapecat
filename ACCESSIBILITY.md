# Accessibility

Scrapecat aims to be usable with a keyboard and common assistive technology.

## Supported environments

- Latest two stable versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
- Keyboard-only navigation.
- Browser zoom up to 200%.

## Known limitations

- The interface is desktop-first; small-screen layouts are basic.
- AI-generated answers and reports may include Mermaid diagrams, math, tables, code, and images. Mermaid diagrams render as SVG with only a generic label and no text alternative, and generated images may lack alt text.
- A full screen-reader and WCAG audit has not been completed.

## Reporting a barrier

Open an issue at <https://github.com/franciscoluna-28/commits-ai/issues> with:

- the page or flow
- steps to reproduce
- the browser and assistive technology used
- the expected behavior

Do not include secrets or private repository data.
